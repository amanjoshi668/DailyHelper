# DailyHelper — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 21, 2026  
**App Name:** DailyHelper (`com.dumbmonk.dailyhelper`)

DailyHelper connects households and small businesses with local independent blue-collar professionals (electricians, plumbers, carpenters, appliance technicians, and painters).

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, phone number, and chosen role (`Customer` or `Professional`) when you register or sign in with Google.
- **Booking & Service Information:** Service PIN code, booking address, appointment date/slot, and repair notes shared strictly between the customer and selected professional.
- **Professional Storefront Data:** Trade skills, serviceable PIN codes, visit fee, hourly rate, optional UPI ID for direct customer QR payment, and personal Khata earnings totals.
- **Voluntary Bug Reports:** Issue descriptions submitted under `Settings -> Report a Bug` (along with page context and app version) to help us fix product defects.

## 2. Payments
DailyHelper facilitates 100% direct Cash or UPI settlement between customers and professionals upon job completion (`0%` commission) and does not collect or store credit/debit card numbers or bank login credentials.

## 3. Android Permissions
- `INTERNET`: Syncing bookings, worker profiles, notifications, and bug reports.
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` & `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`: Delivering booking status alerts and 60-minute local appointment reminders via `AlarmManager`.

## 4. Data Isolation, Security & Deletion
All local device storage is strictly namespaced per user ID and cleared from memory upon sign-out. Passwords are encrypted with salted `scrypt` hashes, and all cloud traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Users may export their records or request complete account deletion at any time.
